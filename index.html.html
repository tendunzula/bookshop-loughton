A traditional independent bookshop on Loughton High Road. Come in for a novel, stay because there is a whole toy shop behind the shelves.

Opening hours and address Order books online

Ask us. We have probably read it.

The shelves are packed, the parquet floor creaks, and someone behind the counter can point you straight to the thing you half-remember the title of.

Fiction and non-fiction for adults, picture books and early readers, teenage fiction, local history and maps, puzzles, cards and stationery. If a book is in print we can order it, usually for the next day.

Order online through Bookshop.org and the shop still gets its share of the sale.

  • Local interest and Essex history

  • National Book Tokens
  • Special orders, usually next day

Keep walking. There is more.

Past the last bookcase the shop opens into the toy shop — the bit children make straight for and adults are quietly pleased about.

Proper toys that last, from the makers you already trust, plus the small things that fix a rainy afternoon.

Birthday sorted in ten minutes
